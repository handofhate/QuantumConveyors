# Settings

In `BepInEx/config/tylerun.moonsweepers.quantumconveyors.cfg` (or your mod manager), section `[Quantum Conveyors]`:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `Adapter Price` | 5 | The build price of each Quantum adapter, in dollars (1 to 1000). Needs a restart |
| `Diagnostics` | off | Verbose troubleshooting lines in the BepInEx log. Turn it on only while chasing a problem |
| `Brain Fart Naming` | off | Swaps the names of the two adapters. Proper semantics is now optional |

Section `[Crew]`:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `Crew changes` | Off | When you host: whether crew-mates may change channels. Off: they only see them. Ask: you allow or deny each change in your E menu. Auto: made at once. Also the **Crew Changes** button in your E menu ([[Crew Games]]) |

Older versions' `Enable Diagnostics` is read as `Diagnostics`.
