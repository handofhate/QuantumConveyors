# Crew Games

- **The host moves the capsules.** Adapters on crew-mates' games show the same channels, but the host's game does the
  transfers.
- **Crew-mates see the host's channels**: every channel, which adapter is on which, the groups and Auto Group. Their
  game asks the host for all of it when the world loads and each time they open the E menu.
- **Crew-mates change channels only if the host allows it** (**Crew Changes**, at the bottom of the host's E menu, or
  `[Crew] Crew changes` in the settings):
  - **Off** (the default): crew-mates only see the channels; their menu has no buttons to change them.
  - **Ask**: a crew-mate's change goes to the host, who gets a notice in the crew feed and finds it at the top of their
    E menu with **Allow** and **Deny**.
  - **Auto**: a crew-mate's change is made at once.
  A crew-mate the game's crew limits stop from building (Moon Sweepers 1.0.6: hold Tab, Build) is always refused.
- **Channel changes reach everyone**, whoever made them: new channels, renames, groups and assignments.
- Everyone who builds or uses the adapters needs the mod installed, the same version as the host.
- Saves from 1.x load unchanged, so an existing crew save keeps its channels.
